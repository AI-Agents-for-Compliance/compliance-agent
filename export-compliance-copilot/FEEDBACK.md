# Feedback session plan

## Goal

Test whether a narrow, traceable workflow for collecting export-case facts and preparing a human compliance review solves a real coordination problem. This is product discovery, not a legal review or sales pitch.

## Before the session

- Use only the built-in synthetic cases and fictional policy rules.
- Ask the participant to avoid sharing confidential or personally identifiable information.
- Explain that all rule outcomes are illustrative and not legal advice.
- Have the participant choose a sample case, inspect the gaps, and record a human review.

## Prompts

1. Walk me through the last time a case like this needed review. What happened first?
2. Which missing fact or document most often blocks the process?
3. Which checks are objective, and where does an expert need to exercise judgment?
4. Are the rule explanation and source reference enough to understand why a case was flagged?
5. At what point should the system ask a person a question or hand the case over?
6. What information belongs in an audit log for your team?
7. What part of this prototype would you not trust? Why?
8. What one workflow would you test next, and what would count as improvement?

## Capture

For each observation, note the workflow step, current workaround, consequence, frequency, and the participant's own words where appropriate. Do not record organization-specific facts, names, or live transaction data in this demo repository.

## Success signal

The session identifies one repeated, costly bottleneck, a realistic source for its evidence, a clear human decision point, and a measurable pilot outcome. Interest in AI alone is not a validation signal.
